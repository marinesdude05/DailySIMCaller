# Daily SIM Caller — Easy Phone Setup

**Version 1.3.18 | Employee guide | One step at a time**

## Before you start

- Ask your supervisor for the approved Daily SIM Caller app and phone.
- Turn on the phone. Connect to the internet.
- Open **Daily SIM Caller**. Do not tap **Call Now** until you are told to start calls.
- Only use the phone numbers your company approved.

## Set up the phone

1. Tap **Help Me Set Up**.
2. **Step 1: Try Shut Down Helper.** Tap **Open Accessibility**. Find **Daily SIM Caller**, then **Shut Down Helper**. Try to turn it on.
3. If Android says access is restricted, tap **Close**, return to Daily SIM Caller, and tap **Next**.
4. **Step 2: Fix Permissions.** Tap **Open App Info**. If you see three dots at the top, tap them, then tap **Allow restricted settings**. Come back and tap **Next**. If you see no three dots, go back to Step 1 and try again.
5. **Step 3: Turn On Helper.** Tap **Open Accessibility** again. Turn on **Shut Down Helper**. Come back and tap **Check Helper**. If it is still off, try **Fix Permissions** again.
6. **Step 4: Restart Phone.** The Android power menu may open. Tap **Restart** yourself. If it does not open, hold the phone's power button to restart. The app will not choose Restart for you.
7. After the phone starts, unlock it and open **Daily SIM Caller**.

## Check the buttons

- **Set Phone App:** If asked, pick **Daily SIM Caller** as the Phone app.
- **Set Home App:** If your supervisor needs calls after a restart, pick **Daily SIM Caller** as the Home app. **This changes your normal Home screen.** Ask for help if you are not sure.
- **Fix Permissions:** Opens Android's app settings. **Opened** means you used the button. It does NOT mean permission is allowed.
- **✓ Done:** Android confirmed that setting is on.
- **Power Menu:** Shows Android's power menu. It does not turn off the phone by itself.
- **Shut Down Helper:** Android must allow this before the special power-off option can work.

## Make calls (only when told to)

1. Check that you have a signal and internet.
2. Make sure the approved number list has loaded. If it has not, ask for help.
3. Tap **Call Now** to start a manual call run.
4. Watch **Call status**. **Speaker ON / OFF** can be changed during a call.
5. If something is wrong, ask your supervisor for help. **Stop Current Queue** is under **For Admin Only**.

## Important

- **Call Now** is manual. It does **not** set a daily reminder.
- Do **not** turn on **Power off after last queued call** unless your supervisor says to.
- Do **not** change **Calls After Restart** unless your supervisor says to.
- If you see an Android warning, stop and ask for help instead of guessing.
- Never call a phone number that is not approved for this work.

**Need help?** Tell your supervisor which step number you are on and what the screen says.

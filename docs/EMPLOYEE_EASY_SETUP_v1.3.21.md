# Daily SIM Caller — Easy Setup (v1.3.21 test version)

**This is a test version. Ask your supervisor before installing or placing calls.**

1. Open **Daily SIM Caller**. Tap **Help Me Set Up**.
2. When Android asks, tap **Allow** or select **Daily SIM Caller**. Return to the app between screens. Follow the permission list **from top to bottom** until the required rows show green **✓ Done**.
3. If Android blocks **Shut Down Helper**, tap **Fix Permissions** right under **Help Me Set Up**. Look for the big flashing **⋮** tip in the app, then tap **Open App Info**.
4. On Android **App info**, look at the **top-right corner** for **⋮** (three dots). If it appears, tap **⋮** and choose **Allow restricted settings**. Return to the app. (If there are no three dots or the option is missing, ask your supervisor.)
5. Tap **Shut Down Helper**. In Android Accessibility, choose Daily SIM Caller and turn on the helper. Return and check for the green **✓**.
6. **Power Menu** only opens Android's power menu; do not tap Power off or Restart unless told to.
7. Tap **Call Now** only when your supervisor says to. Use approved phone numbers only.

**Green ✓ means approved. Orange ! means check that setting.** The app cannot approve permissions or press Android's three dots for you.
